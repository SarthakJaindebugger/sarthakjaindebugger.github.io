A cross-platform natural-language assistant for navigating structured and unstructured parliamentary records, built during an internship at EY.

**Stack:** Python, LLM fine-tuning, RAG, FAISS, LangChain, Hugging Face, NLP, data mining.

SansadGPT combines retrieval and generation over large document corpora, cutting manual search time through heavyweight PDFs by **85%**.
