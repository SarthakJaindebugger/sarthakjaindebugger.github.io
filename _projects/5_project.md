Multimodal neuro-signal processing pipelines designed to support early Autism Spectrum Disorder screening and clinical-trial workflows.

**Stack:** Qwen3-4B-Thinking, Llama Maverick, Llama Scout, LLaMA 3, Gemini 2.5 Pro, Gemma-3n-E4B-it, reproducible evaluation.

The proposed fine-tuned model achieved **0.843 accuracy** and **0.891 F1**, with model-comparison reports and deployment guidance to support product decisions.
