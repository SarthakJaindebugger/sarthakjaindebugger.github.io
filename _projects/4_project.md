**ViBe** is a benchmark of 3,500+ videos for evaluating visual hallucinations in text-to-video diffusion models, developed with the University of South Carolina AIISC.

**Methods:** VideoMAE, TimeSformer, video understanding, hallucination mitigation, multimodal evaluation.

This work was published at **ACL 2025 (TrustNLP)** and turns a difficult qualitative reliability problem into a measurable research task.
